The DEXA-PSYCH trial

Dexamethasone for moderate-to-severe depression—a double-blind, randomized, parallel-group, placebo-controlled trial

The DEXA-PSYCH trial is an investigator-initiated, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel group superiority trial. Three-hundred participants meeting criteria for moderate-to-severe depression were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to 1 week of either dexamethasone or placebo as add-on treatment to treatment as usual.

Sponsor Institution: Copenhagen Research Centre for Biological and Precision Psychiatry, Mental Health Centre Copenhagen, Copenhagen University Hospital—Bispebjerg and Frederiksberg, Copenhagen, Denmark.

Sponsor and Principal Investigator: Michael Eriksen Benros.

Status

Per date: 2026-11-08

  • Enrollment: Done. 300 participants randomized.
  • Completion: Main trial (up to day 28 visit) is complete. Six-month follow-up is on-going.

For further details, see the timeline.

Background

Depression is one of the leading causes of years lived with disability, globally. The effects of current pharmacological treatments are small-to-moderate and often delayed by weeks. Immunological disturbances have been associated with depression and meta-analyses have suggested that anti-inflammatory agents have moderate-to-large anti-depressant effects. The largest effects were reported for glucocorticoids. However, previous trials were too small to legitimize standard use of glucocorticoids as add-on treatment in depression.

Purpose

The purpose of the DEXA-PSYCH trial was to investigate the effect and safety of short-term, oral dexamethasone compared to placebo as add-on therapy in moderate-to-severe depression.

Protocol and Trial registration

The DEXA-PSYCH trial has EU Trial registration number 2022-501428-45-00 with WHO universal Trial Identifier U1111-1280-7614.

The Trial protocol is available here in its latest version and an earlier version is presented in Rømer et al (2025).

Statistical Analysis Plan

The Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) was archived and locked in a persistent and immutable Zenodo DOI prior to unblinding of the randomization list. The time of unblinding coincides with locking of the database and is called database lock (DBL).

Analysis code

All code to produce the statistical analyses is openly shared on the DEXA-PSYCH GitHub repository.

Analyses were programmed with dummy treatment-group identifiers, and the code was archived before unblinding. The primary analysis of the primary endpoint was independently parallel programmed by two separate statisticians.

The README file describes the structure and contents of the code base.

The Change log describes the evolution and maintenance of the code base and new releases of trial results.

Timeline

The key timeline for the main trial is as follows:

  • 2021-12-09: Grant awarded from the Novo Nordisk Foundation
  • 2022-07-25: Pre-registration on the EU Clinical Trials Information System and submission for authority approval
  • 2022-10-21: Approval granted by the Danish Medicines Agency and the Danish Health Research Ethics Committee
  • 2022-12-28: Recruitment initiated
  • 2023-01-13: First patient randomized
  • 2026-04-16: Last version of the protocol (version 1.4) approved by the authorities and published
  • 2026-10-12: Day-28 visit of the last patient
  • 2026-10-19: Final external monitoring by the Good Clinical Practice (GCP) unit
  • 2026-10-20: Statistical analysis plan locked in Zenodo DOI
  • 2026-10-21: Database locked, Statistical analysis code finalized and locked in Zenodo DOI
  • 2026-10-22: Randomization list received: unblinding of statisticians, principal investigator and selected sub-investigators
  • 2026-10-23: All Pre-specified analyses run and results archived in Zenodo DOI and post-unblinding code archived in Zenodo DOI
  • 2026-mm-dd: Primary publication published

As of October 2026 three assessors remain unblinded to collect 6-month follow-up data. The last follow-up data is expected to be collected March 2027.
